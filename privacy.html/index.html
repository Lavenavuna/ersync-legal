Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ERSync Digital ("ERSync Digital", "we", "us"), a Fiji-based AI automation and digital services consultancy, collects, uses, and protects information, including information accessed through connected third-party platforms such as TikTok, Meta/Facebook, and Instagram.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide directly, such as name and email address.
  • Platform data accessed via official APIs when you authorize a connection — for example, your TikTok display name, avatar, and the minimum account information required to publish or schedule content on your behalf.
  • Content you supply for publishing, such as images, video, and captions.

2. How We Use TikTok Platform Data

When you authorize ERSync Digital's application to connect to your TikTok account, we request only the scopes necessary to publish or manage content you explicitly submit (e.g. the Content Posting API's video.publish scope). We do not sell TikTok user data, use it for advertising profiles unrelated to your own account, or share it with third parties except infrastructure providers who help us operate the service (e.g. our automation/API middleware provider) under confidentiality obligations. We retain TikTok access tokens only as long as needed to provide the connected service, and you may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately ends our access.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to: operate and improve our services; publish or schedule content you authorize; communicate with you about your account or services; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with: (a) infrastructure and API middleware providers necessary to operate connected-platform features, under contractual confidentiality terms; and (b) authorities where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or until you request deletion, whichever is earlier.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, and may revoke any third-party platform authorization at any time through that platform's own settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, including encrypted transmission of API credentials.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details listed on ersyncdigital.com.
